Our Story

Where Chennai Celebrates
Its Most Cherished Moments

Nestled in the heart of Shenoy Nagar, LKN Banquet has been the chosen stage for thousands of Tamil Nadu families — a place where tradition is honoured, joy is shared, and every celebration is treated as truly unique.

From the sacred vows of a Muhurtham to the laughter of a birthday morning, our 350-seat air-conditioned main hall and 150-seat dining hall have welcomed families from across Chennai and beyond. We believe every event deserves an atmosphere that feels both grand and intimate — a space that amplifies the warmth of the occasion, not just host it.

Located on Pulla Avenue — just a 3-minute walk from Shenoy Nagar Metro and directly accessible from the main road — we make it easy for every guest to arrive comfortably, and leave with memories they will carry for a lifetime.

— The LKN Banquet Family, Shenoy Nagar, Chennai
